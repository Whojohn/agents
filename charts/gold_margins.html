Method. Financial statements are the input; AISC is the checksum. For every company-quarter the same extracted line items are re-aggregated per the World Gold Council build-up and tied back to the company's own published AISC — a residual above 2% means the filing was misread and the observation is rejected. All 88 observations passed. Mean absolute residual by company: Kinross 0.02%, Newmont 0.04%, Barrick 0.14%, Agnico 0.22%; worst single observation 1.30%.

Smoothing. Rolling 8-quarter window; the top and bottom ⌊0.15 × 8⌋ = 1 observation is dropped from each tail and the middle six averaged. A trim discards extremes rather than capping them, so one catastrophic quarter cannot drag the line. Every dropped observation is logged to trimmed_observations.csv.

Validation. Implied realised price (gold revenue ÷ ounces sold) converges across all four independently extracted datasets to within ~1% in every quarter — no external gold price was used anywhere in this pipeline. The AISC margin exceeds the all-in margin in all 88 observations, as it must.

Not shown. AngloGold Ashanti and Gold Fields publish full financial statements only half-yearly and are excluded from this quarterly view rather than interpolated.
